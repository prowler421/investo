# M2 — Testing

CLAUDE.md § Testing expectations governs, and three of its rules do most of the work here:

- **Assert the derivation, not the value.** M2 is nothing but derivations, and almost every one of
  them has a wrong version that agrees with the right one on the fixture in front of you.
- **For any sentence of the form "X cannot happen", write the test that attempts X.**
- **Boundaries get their own test.**

DESIGN §11 is normative on approach: *"Golden fixtures: real `companyfacts` JSON for ~15 companies
with known-hard cases … Assert exact expected series."* Two words in that sentence are doing work
M2 cannot currently satisfy — **real**, and **exact** — see [§ 2](#2-fixtures).

## 2. Fixtures

### What the existing set already proves, and it is more than expected

Every fixture in `tests/fixtures/edgar/` is synthetic, generated by `make_fixtures.py`, with faithful
shapes and invented content. That is a limitation for the coverage measurement and **not** for the
rules — a rule about `filed`-date ordering is as testable against an invented payload as a real one,
provided the shape is right.

| Fixture | The M2 rule it settles |
|---|---|
| `RESTATER` | [`02-facts.md` § 1](02-facts.md#1-the-pipeline-order-and-why-it-is-not-negotiable) — `as_of` before dedup. Four `filed` dates, four values, one period; `--as-of 2021-06-30` must yield 812,000,000 and dedup-first yields nothing. |
| `NOQ4` | [`02-facts.md` § 6](02-facts.md#6-q4-derivation) — conditional Q4. FY2022 derives 301M; FY2023 must derive nothing. Both halves in one payload, which is what makes the unconditional rule fail visibly. |
| `AAPL` | [`01-tags.md` § 1](01-tags.md#1-resolution-is-period-wise-and-this-is-the-decision-the-milestone-turns-on) — the ASC 606 stitch, with `SalesRevenueNet` on FY2016–17 and the 606 tag on FY2018–19, so series-level resolution loses two of four years. Also the equal-value re-filing (2019-06-29 under four accessions) and `391035000000.01` for the `Decimal` round-trip. |
| `IPO` | `quarters_available == 6`, on the wrong side of §5.1's 12-quarter boundary, and an annual series that is entirely empty — so Q4 derivation has no `whole` to subtract from. |
| `BANK`, `REIT` | Chain misses that must produce a populated history and a coverage report, not an exception: no `OperatingIncomeLoss` on either, no capex tag on `REIT`. |
| `ARXS` | No `dei` section, an unanticipated `ffd` taxonomy, `pure`-unit decimals, `fy: null`, instants with `start` absent. Its submissions payload holds **one `10-Q` and no `10-K`** (`sic: 3728`, `files: []`), so its annual spine is empty while its quarterly spine has one entry — `fill_rate is None` on every annual metric ([`03-statements.md` § 2](03-statements.md#2-the-period-spine)). |

### Six gaps in the synthetic set, and they are M2's to close

Each is a rule this design states that no existing payload can exercise. These are coding work
inside M2, not research, and they land with the module they test:

| New fixture | Why the existing set cannot do it |
|---|---|
| `YTDONLY` | **No fixture contains a YTD fact at all.** Every payload has discrete quarters. So [`02-facts.md` § 7](02-facts.md#7-ytd-differencing) is currently unfalsifiable, and a differencing implementation that never fires passes every test. Needs a filer with cumulative-only 10-Q presentation: 3M, 6M, 9M and FY, no discrete quarters. |
| `TIER2` | **No fixture contains a single tier-2 tag.** No COGS, SG&A, D&A, interest, SBC, receivables, retained earnings, current assets or current liabilities appear anywhere in `tests/fixtures/`. ROADMAP M2's exit criterion is stated on both tiers; half of it is presently untested by anything. Also carries SG&A split into `GeneralAndAdministrativeExpense` + `SellingAndMarketingExpense`, which is the one non-substitution member ([`01-tags.md` § 4](01-tags.md#4-tier-2--what-the-fzm-scores-need)). |
| `NCI` | `LiabilitiesAndStockholdersEquity` and `StockholdersEquityIncludingPortionAttributableToNoncontrollingInterest` with a **material** NCI, and no `Liabilities` tag — the branch [`01-tags.md` § 9](01-tags.md#9-the-equity-trap-in-the-liabilities-derivation) exists for. The test asserts the derived liabilities differ from `L&SE − StockholdersEquity` by exactly the NCI, which is the only assertion that distinguishes the right derivation from the tempting one. |
| `STUBYEAR` | A fiscal-year change: a transition period in `OTHER` (say 140 days) between two `ANNUAL` years, plus a 53-week year at 371 days. Pins both sides of [`02-facts.md` § 4](02-facts.md#4-bucketing-and-the-two-questions-m1-deferred) — the stub drops and is counted, the 53-week year does not. |
| `BADUNIT` | `EarningsPerShareDiluted` tagged under `USD`, and a revenue fact under `EUR`. [`01-tags.md` § 7](01-tags.md#7-units)'s filter is otherwise asserted only by its absence. |
| `NOPERIODIC` | **No fixture produces an `OBSERVED` spine.** `ARXS` was the obvious candidate and turns out to have a 10-Q, so its spine origin is `FILINGS` with an empty annual bucket. Needs a submissions payload whose forms are `S-1/A` and `8-K` only, paired with a companyfacts payload that has facts — which is the shape that makes the circular denominator reachable at all. |

The gaps were established by parsing every payload in `tests/fixtures/edgar/companyfacts/`: no
fact anywhere has a duration in 101–349 days or outside 80–380, no tier-2 tag appears in any file,
neither NCI tag appears, and the only units present are `USD`, `USD/shares`, `shares` and `pure`.
Each gap is a claim that was checked rather than assumed, and the check is one command.

A seventh case would be an exclusivity-group conflict — both assessed-tax revenue variants on
overlapping periods — and it can go in `TIER2` rather than earning its own payload. So can
`GrossProfit` alongside COGS, since no fixture has either and the derivation needs both branches.

### The curated real set, and what it gates

`tests/fixtures/edgar/PROVENANCE.md` holds the fetch commands; ROADMAP records the ~3-day curation
workstream as **not done**. This design is written against the synthetic set deliberately, so
implementation is not blocked on research — but two things are honestly unavailable until curation
lands, and neither should be quietly assumed:

1. **DESIGN §11's "assert exact expected series" is currently self-referential.** Asserting that
   Apple's FY2018 revenue is 265,595,000,000 against a payload that says so because
   `make_fixtures.py` was told to say so tests the fixture generator. The rules above are tested by
   asserting *derivations* — that FY2023 yields four quarters and not five, that the NCI
   derivation differs from the naive one by the NCI — which is why they survive the synthetic set
   intact. Value assertions do not, and should not be written as though they do.
2. **ROADMAP M2's exit criterion is not assessable.** See [§ 3](#3-the-coverage-measurement).

## 3. The coverage measurement

ROADMAP M2's exit: *"≥90% coverage across 20 NASDAQ names on **both** the DCF metric set and the
quality-score metric set."* Three things have to exist before that sentence is a gate rather than
an aspiration.

**A denominator.** [`03-statements.md` § 2](03-statements.md#2-the-period-spine). Without it,
"90% coverage" is 90% of an unstated quantity, and the three plausible readings differ by more
than the margin being measured.

**A universe that is not 20 large caps.** Tag coverage correlates with filer size — large filers
have accounting departments that tag completely, and §4.2's own capex entry says under half of
filers tag the preferred concept. Twenty NASDAQ-100 names would measure ~97% and predict nothing
about the twenty-first company someone runs. The universe is therefore **stratified**: four names
from each market-cap quintile of the NASDAQ listing, with at least one bank (SIC 6000–6499), one
REIT, and one filer with under three years of history, and with the seven fixture companies
excluded so the measurement is out-of-sample against the rules that were written looking at them.

**A harness that cannot run in CI.** CLAUDE.md convention 7: CI sets no `INVESTO_*` variables, and
a test that reaches the network must fail rather than quietly succeed. So this is
`tests/coverage_probe.py`, marked `network`, deselected by default, run by a person:

```
uv run pytest -m network tests/coverage_probe.py
```

It prints a per-metric fill-rate table for both tiers, annual and quarterly, across the universe,
and the run is recorded in **`docs/m2/COVERAGE.md`** — the date, the twenty tickers, the per-metric
figures, and the two tier aggregates. That file is the evidence for the exit criterion, in the same
way `PROVENANCE.md` is the evidence for the fixtures: a claim with a procedure attached that
someone else can repeat.

Like fixture curation, this has **no green test to declare it finished**, which is how it silently
eats a week while the code looks complete. Sized separately in
[README § Sizing](README.md#9-sizing).

**What to do if it comes in under 90%** is a decision, not a bug to fix, and it should be made
against the table rather than by adding chain members until the number moves. §4.2's own entity
counts say some metrics cannot reach 90% — `LongTermDebtNoncurrent` is 1,532 filers of ~5,000, and
§4.2 calls the chain "the weakest of the set. Expect misses; mark leverage metrics
low-confidence." A gate that forces 90% on that metric is a gate that will be met by a chain member
that means something slightly different. The honest form of the criterion is per-metric floors with
the weak ones named, and if the measurement supports that, it is a ROADMAP amendment rather than a
code change.

## 4. New layering rules

`tests/test_layering.py` currently scopes every rule to `ingest/` or `domain/`, so `normalize/` and
`report/` arrive unconstrained. Six additions, in the same AST-over-the-installed-package form, each
with the allowlist pinned by its own test so a future exception is a visible edit.

```python
USGAAP_LITERAL_ALLOWED: Final = {
    "normalize/tags.py": "the chain registry: the single home for every us-gaap tag",
}
```

| Rule | Scope | Why |
|---|---|---|
| A `us-gaap` literal appears **only** in `normalize/tags.py` | whole package | M1 forbade it in `ingest/` because *"a tag literal in `ingest/` is the first line of a second, shadow copy of `normalize/tags.py`."* The same argument applies to `analyze/`, `report/` and `backtest/` and is stronger there, because those modules have a plausible reason to want one. The converse test also holds: `tags.py` must contain them, or the rule is passing because the registry moved. |
| No clock read under `normalize/**` or `report/**` | those trees | Extends `test_no_now_call_in_parsers` past `ingest/`. `as_of` is resolved at the command boundary and threaded down ([`02-facts.md` § 2](02-facts.md#2-as_of)); a `date.today()` in the pipeline makes two runs either side of midnight differ, and §11 would report it as nondeterminism rather than as the design mistake it is. **Empty allowlist**, unlike `ingest/`'s. |
| `normalize/**` imports no `httpx`, `urllib`, `socket`, `requests`, `sqlite3` | `normalize/**` | The same `_DOMAIN_FORBIDDEN` set already applied to `domain/`. A normalization layer that can fetch is one that will, and then the warm-run guarantee is gone. |
| `normalize/**` does not import `investo.report`, `investo.analyze` or `investo.backtest` | `normalize/**` | §3's one-directional flow. `report/serialize.py` importing `normalize` is correct; the reverse is not. |
| No `sorted(...)` or `.sort()` without `key=` under `normalize/**` or `report/**` | those trees | [`02-facts.md` § 9](02-facts.md#9-every-sort-key-must-be-total). `FiscalPeriod` compares on `(end, kind)` and is not a total order over facts; a stable sort over a partial key returns input order for ties, and input order descends from dict iteration over a parsed payload. Deterministic today, not a guarantee, and invisible when wrong. |
| No `float` constructed under `normalize/**` or `report/**` | those trees | CLAUDE.md convention 8, at the layer where the temptation appears: a fill rate looks like a `float` and `json.dumps` accepts one. AST rule on `float(...)` calls and on float literals. |

`report/serialize.py` needs no `pathlib` allowance because **it returns a string and does not
write** — the command owns the file. That keeps the serializer pure and makes the determinism test
a string comparison rather than a filesystem fixture.

## 6. Determinism, at M2's scope

§11's gate is a byte-identical PDF and M2 renders none. What M2 can gate, and what M3 inherits:

- **`report.json` is byte-identical across two runs on one cache.** [`04-serialize.md` § 5](04-serialize.md#5-determinism).
  This is the first artifact the gate applies to, and getting the rules settled here means M3 adds
  the PDF to an existing gate rather than building one.
- **The document is a function of the cache, not the clock.** A `--refresh` between runs changes
  the bytes, because `fetched_at` moved and the run saw different data. That is the gate working.
- **`PYTHONHASHSEED` does not change the output.** Asserted across a subprocess boundary, because
  the in-process assertion cannot see a `frozenset` iteration order that is fixed for the life of
  the interpreter — and `CompanyFacts` carries two `frozenset` fields.
- **Fact ordering within a series is total.** [`02-facts.md` § 9](02-facts.md#9-every-sort-key-must-be-total),
  enforced by the AST rule and asserted by shuffling the input facts and comparing serialized output.

The `SOURCE_DATE_EPOCH` / `svg.hashsalt` / `metadata={"Date": None}` list from §9.0 is M3's and is
not started here.

## 8. What M2 does not test, and why

- **Whether the chains are *right* for the long tail of filers.** [§ 3](#3-the-coverage-measurement)
  measures it against twenty names and records the result; twenty stratified names is evidence, not
  proof, and §4.2's own entity counts say some metrics cannot reach the criterion. The tier-2
  orderings in [`01-tags.md` § 4](01-tags.md#4-tier-2--what-the-fzm-scores-need) are this
  document's proposals and are the weakest-evidenced part of it.
- **Exact expected series against real filings.** [§ 2](#2-fixtures). Blocked on curation; the
  rules are tested by their derivations instead, which is what makes the block tolerable rather
  than fatal.
- **§6.10's SIC branch end-to-end.** `BANK` and `REIT` have no submissions payload, so their SIC
  is in `PROVENANCE.md` rather than in anything M2 reads. `test_statements` covers the SIC gate
  with a synthesized `CompanyProfile`; the join from a real payload is untested until the fixture
  gap closes, and that gap is already recorded.
- **Whether the coverage floor in config is set correctly.** §4.2 says coverage below "a
  configurable floor" degrades confidence. M2 measures coverage and emits the
  `coverage_below_floor` finding; what the floor *should be* is answerable only from
  [§ 3](#3-the-coverage-measurement)'s table, and picking one before measuring would be picking the
  number that makes today's fixtures pass.
- **Anything about the PDF.** M3.
